Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · Applies to the xPST application and this website.

xPST is a local-first application. It runs on your computer, uses your own accounts, and stores your data on your device. We do not operate a service that receives your content, and we do not collect analytics or telemetry from the application.

1. Who we are

xPST is an open-source project distributed under the MIT or Apache-2.0 licence. The source code, issue tracker and documentation are hosted at github.com/TysAIs/xPST. There is no company operating a hosted xPST service, and no account is required to use the software.

2. What the application stores, and where

All application data stays on the device you install it on:

  • Platform credentials. When you connect a social account, xPST performs the platform's own authorisation flow and stores the resulting session or token in a local configuration directory (~/.xpst/ on macOS and Linux). Credentials are encrypted at rest; no plaintext credential is written to disk.
  • Posting history and state. A local record of what was posted, where, and what the platform returned, so the tool can avoid duplicate posts and retry failures.
  • Configuration and logs. Your settings, and logs used for troubleshooting. Logs are designed to exclude personal data and credentials.

We have no access to any of this. It never leaves your machine except in the direct API calls you initiate to the platforms you have connected.

3. What we do not collect

  • No analytics or usage telemetry is sent to us.
  • No crash reports are uploaded automatically.
  • No personal identifiers are embedded in the software or its repository.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your video content, captions, or account data.

4. What is sent to third parties

When you publish, xPST sends data directly from your device to the platform you chose using that platform's official API. That is the only transfer that happens, and it is initiated by you. For example, publishing to YouTube or Instagram sends the video and caption you selected to that platform, using your own authorised account. When xPST downloads a video from TikTok as a source, the transfer runs the other way: content is read from TikTok onto your machine, and xPST does not upload anything back to TikTok.

The data each platform receives, and how that platform uses it, is governed by that platform's own privacy policy. xPST does not act as an intermediary and never receives a copy.

5. Permissions xPST requests, and why

When you connect a platform, xPST asks only for the permissions needed to do the job you asked for:

Platform and scopeWhy it is requested
TikTok — user.info.basic Read the authorised user's basic profile (open id, display name, avatar) so the application can confirm which TikTok account is connected and label it in the interface. Stored only in local configuration.
TikTok — user.info.stats Display the connected account's own follower, like and video counts in the application's local analytics view, so the creator can see their own performance. Read-only, own account only.
TikTok — video.publish Requested by the TikTok app registration for the pending destination path. xPST does not offer TikTok publishing today: until TikTok approves an audited application, that API only permits private (self-only) posts for at most 5 users per 24 hours, and xPST reports the capability as blocked rather than advertising it. The permission is not exercised by the current release.
TikTok — video.upload Requested for the same pending destination path, to upload the user's own video as a draft to the account's inbox so the creator can finish captions or edits inside the TikTok application. It is not used by the current release.
YouTube, X, Instagram The equivalent posting, reading and analytics permissions for your own accounts on those platforms, requested through each platform's own authorisation flow.

You can revoke any of these at any time from the platform's own settings, or with xpst disconnect <platform>, which removes the stored credentials from your device.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because data is stored locally, retention is yours to control. Removing the application's configuration directory deletes the credentials, posting history and logs. The command xpst disconnect <platform> removes a single platform's stored credentials without touching the rest. There is no server-side copy for us to delete.

7. This website

This website is served by GitHub Pages as static files. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and performs no tracking. GitHub's own logging as the hosting provider is described in GitHub's privacy statement.

8. Children

xPST is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone below the minimum age required to hold an account on the platforms it connects to.

9. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published on this page with an updated date, and recorded in the repository's history at github.com/TysAIs/xPST.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at github.com/TysAIs/xPST/issues.
