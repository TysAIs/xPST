Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

xPST is a local-first desktop application. Unless you enable remote scheduling features, all processing happens on your own machine: your content is uploaded from your computer directly to the platforms you authorize (YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok) using each platform's official APIs.

What we store

xPST stores only local configuration and OAuth credentials on your device (in ~/.xpst/ by default): platform access tokens, channel IDs, and posting history/state. Tokens are stored encrypted at rest when possible.

What leaves your device

Only the content you choose to publish and the API calls needed to publish and check status (video metadata, captions, and refresh-token exchanges). xPST does not sell data, show ads, or track you. No analytics are sent to xPST servers.
